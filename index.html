FAQ

Before you blast off

Does this work on my phone?

No — Space Shooter is built exclusively for Wear OS smartwatches. Install it from the Play Store on your watch (or via the Wear OS / Play Store companion flow on your phone).

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. What you see on Play is what you get.

Which control method should I use?

If your watch has a rotary crown or bezel, start there — most players find it the most precise. Touch works on every watch. Tilt is great when you want hands-free steering after a quick calibration.

Does the game collect my data?

No. The Play Store Data safety section lists no data collected and no data shared with third parties.

Is there a high-score leaderboard online?

Scores are local. Your all-time best lives on the watch — the rival is your previous run.
