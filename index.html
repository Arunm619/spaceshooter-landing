FAQ

Objections, answered

Does this work on my phone?

No — Space Shooter is Wear OS only. Install from Play on your watch, or via the Wear OS / Play companion flow on your phone.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads. No in-app purchases. What you see on Play is what you get.

Which control should I start with?

Rotary crown if you have one — most precise. Touch works everywhere. Tilt after a quick calibration when you want hands-free steer.

Does the game collect my data?

No. Play’s Data safety section lists no data collected and no data shared with third parties.

Is there an online leaderboard?

Scores stay on the watch. Your all-time best is the only rival that matters.
